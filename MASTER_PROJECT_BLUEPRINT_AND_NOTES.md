## 3. The Hybrid Quality System: AI Speed + Human Review

We rejected the amateur "100% unchecked AI" model because parents paying for keepsakes demand perfection.

* **Track 1: Curated Signature Stories (Instant Delivery):**
  * 3–5 pre-crafted master story universes where backgrounds, color palettes, and poetic rhyming verses are **100% pre-vetted by human authors**.
  * AI personalizes child likeness, name, and dedications.
  * Delivered in **5 minutes**.
* **Track 2: Bespoke Studio Stories (12–24h Human Review):**
  * Custom storylines based on exact child hobbies.
  * AI drafts the text and illustrations.
  * Enters an internal **Admin QA Dashboard**:
    * Reviewer takes **2 to 3 minutes** to verify Arabic Tashkeel grammar and character visual consistency.
    * Click "Approve & Deliver".
    * Framed to customers as *"Hand-inspected by our editorial studio"*, increasing luxury perception.

## 5. Refund Policy (Custom Goods Exemption)

* Under international consumer law and UAE Consumer Protection Law, **custom personalized products are legally exempt from standard change-of-mind refunds**.
* **Policy Rules:**
  1. *Change of mind:* Non-refundable (clearly stated at checkout).
  2. *Typo made by customer:* Offer a 50% discount coupon to regenerate.
  3. *Zero surprise guarantee:* Customers preview the 3D book *before* paying, reducing dispute rates to under 0.5%.
