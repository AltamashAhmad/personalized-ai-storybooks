### Book 01: The Pearl Diver's Secret Treasure (`the-pearl-divers-secret-treasure`)
* **Theme:** Arabian Gulf maritime history, courage, environmental wonder, honoring ancestors.
* **Hero Role:** The child sets sail on a traditional wooden *Sambook* dhow from Dubai Creek, guided by an ancient nautical sea-map. They dive into shimmering turquoise waters, befriend a sea turtle, explore sunken coral gardens, and retrieve the legendary Rainbow Pearl that brings joy and prosperity to the coastal towns.
* **Target Audience:** Boys & girls ages 3 to 9; perfect for birthdays, Gulf heritage days, and milestone gifts.
* **Spread Structure (12 Full Spreads):**
  1. *Spread 1:* Departure from historic Dubai Creek at dawn as wooden dhows raise white sails.

  7. *Spread 7:* Swimming with playful dolphins (*Al Dulfeen*) toward the sunset horizon.
  8. *Spread 8:* Returning to the wooden deck as the crew celebrates the young captain's bravery.
  9. *Spread 9:* Starlight navigation using the North Star (*Al Najm*) across calm waters.

  12. *Spread 12:* Tucked into bed with a seashell by the pillow, dreaming of the ocean.
