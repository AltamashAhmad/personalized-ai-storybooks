More magical than anything he'd dreamed!

Chapter Two • A Feathered Friend The Hidden Oasis
The compass hummed and pointed to a stone,
Where sat a tiny falcon, all alone.

Its golden wing was caught in desert vine,
Zayed gently smiled and said: "Your wing is fine!"

كَانَ جَنَاحُهُ الذَّهَبِيُّ عَالِقًا بَيْنَ أَغْصَانِ النَّبَاتِ،
