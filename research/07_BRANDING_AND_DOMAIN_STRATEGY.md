# 07. Brand Identity, Naming & Domain Strategy

## The Challenge
The brand must achieve two things simultaneously:
1. **In the UAE & GCC:** Feel deeply authentic, respected, and culturally premium (resonating with Emirati and Arab expat families).
2. **In the US, UK, and Europe:** Feel modern, whimsical, easy to pronounce, and trustworthy (competing directly with Wonderbly and Hooray Heroes).

## 3. Dual-Domain Strategy
* **UAE & GCC Market:** Secure `[brand].ae` (registered via AEserver or Etisalat). A `.ae` domain builds instant local trust with UAE banks, parents, and schools.
* **Global Market:** Secure `[brand].com` or `get[brand].com` on Cloudflare/Namecheap for the programmatic SEO engine targeting US, UK, and Europe.
