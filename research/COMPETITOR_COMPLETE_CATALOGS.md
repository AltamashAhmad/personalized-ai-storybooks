1. **Massive White Space in the Middle East:** Across all 75 cataloged titles from both giants, **not a single title supports Arabic or GCC regional themes**. Our bilingual English/Arabic editions have zero competition.
2. **The 3 Evergreen Pillars to Launch With:**
   - **Pillar 1:** Bedtime Story (Wonderbly's *Bedtime For You* + Hooray Heroes' *Always Little*).
   - **Pillar 2:** Adventurous Animal/Heritage Story (Our *Desert Falcon & Grand Mosque* story).
   - **Pillar 3:** Milestone/Aspiration Story (Our *Little Astronaut Mission*).
3. **In-Funnel Monetization:** Hooray Heroes proves that adding **'+5 Extra Stories for $5.99'** and pre-selecting Hardcover by default increases AOV by over 35%.
